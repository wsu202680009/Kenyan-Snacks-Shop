About us

We started as a small kibanda by the roadside, frying mandazi before sunrise for people on their way to work. Today we still cook everything by hand, in small batches, with the recipes our family has used for years.

Our samosas are filled with spiced minced beef or lentils, our bhajia are sliced thin and crispy, and our mahindi choma is roasted over charcoal and rubbed with lemon and chilli.

  • Fresh dailyCooked every morning, never reheated from yesterday.
  • Local ingredientsMaize, potatoes and coconuts from Kenyan farms.
  • Fair pricesStreet-food prices, so everyone can enjoy a snack.
